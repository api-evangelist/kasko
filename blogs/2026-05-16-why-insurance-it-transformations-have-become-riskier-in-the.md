Insurers are making long-term technology commitments while AI is shortening the certainty cycle around software architecture, vendor models, and build economics. This article explains why insurance IT transformation risk is becoming a governance problem, not just an execution problem, and why incremental modernisation can reduce lock-in by generating evidence earlier.
